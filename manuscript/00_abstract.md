# Abstract {#sec:abstract}

**DAF-OKN: Open Knowledge Network Pull, Analyze, Visualize, Contribute.** This paper documents the `daf-okn` pipeline for the [Open Knowledge Network (OKN)](https://okn.us/): a reproducible, test-backed workflow that pulls the registry graph catalog from [registry.okn.us](https://registry.okn.us/), converts knowledge graphs into a typed graph document model, measures their structure, renders figures, and packages new contributions back to the registry. The implementation lives in `src/daf_okn/` (`core/`, `analysis/`, `figures/`, `dashboard/`), with thin CLI orchestrators in `scripts/`.

The pipeline is demonstrated end to end on the Active Inference Institute `cognitive` knowledge base: an Obsidian vault is exported to a typed graph (`export_cognitive`), measured with structural metrics (`analyze_graph`), and packaged as N-Quads plus a registry entry draft (`provenance record`). Every quantitative claim in this manuscript is a `{{TOKEN}}` hydrated from `output/data/graph_report.json` and `manuscript/config.yaml` by `src/daf_okn/core/manuscript_variables.py` — no number is hardcoded in prose. For the analyzed graph, the report records **{{NODE_COUNT}}** nodes and **{{EDGE_COUNT}}** directed edges at a density of **{{DENSITY_PCT}}%**, spanning **{{WEAKLY_CONNECTED_COMPONENTS}}** weakly and **{{STRONGLY_CONNECTED_COMPONENTS}}** strongly connected components, with **{{TRIANGLE_COUNT}}** triangles and mean degree **{{MEAN_DEGREE}}**; the most central node is `{{TOP_NODE_LABEL}}` (centrality {{TOP_NODE_CENTRALITY}}).

Contributions are **methodological** and **architectural**: (1) a registry-native pull/analyze/visualize/contribute chain over the OKN fabric's documented SPARQL, TPF, and embeddings surfaces; (2) a typed `GraphDocument` model with structural invariants that invalid documents cannot silently pass; and (3) a zero-fabrication token contract in which prose, tables, and figures track the last pipeline run. The registry catalog and the exported knowledge graph are visualized in [@fig:registry_catalog] and [@fig:cognitive_network].
